Maze Escape

A third-person maze escape prototype exploring enemy pursuit, line-of-sight and the player's ability to evade AI guards.

Design objective

Build an escape experience around navigating a maze while avoiding AI guards. The core interaction connects spatial navigation with the pressure of being detected and pursued.

Gameplay systems

The prototype experiments with Unreal Engine behaviour trees, enemy line-of-sight and pursuit. A stamina constraint limits the player's ability to evade enemies, bringing movement decisions into the escape loop.

  • Enemy behaviour: detecting and pursuing the player.
  • Player movement: stamina as a constraint on evasion.
  • Level design: a maze as the setting for navigation and escape.
