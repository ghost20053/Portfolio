Physical–Virtual Interaction

Practice-led research into aligning physical objects with virtual representations for interaction in VR.

Research submitted · Awaiting assessment
Technology
Unreal Engine · Meta Quest 3 · Hand tracking
Focus
Interaction design, calibration and reflective development
Outcome
Research artefact, development journal and developer toolkit

Design question

What design considerations emerge when aligning physical objects with virtual representations for interaction in VR?

The prototype pairs a virtual tabletop scene with physical objects, including a table, cubes and a chest. The original research uses untracked physical props and a controlled starting arrangement.

Calibration and onboarding

Early virtual hand targets could trigger calibration while asking the user to reach away from the real tabletop. The later approach used a two-hand tabletop interaction to connect the starting position with physical contact.

This iteration treated calibration and onboarding as one design problem: the instructions, the physical setup and the system's success condition needed to describe the same action.

Evidence and reflection

Three Action Research cycles documented experiments, technical constraints and design revisions. The supporting toolkit translates those observations into reflective questions about intended action, physical counterparts, correspondence, system knowledge, starting arrangements and onboarding.

These are practice-derived findings from documented development, not independently validated claims about usability or tracking accuracy. Later QR-tracking experiments are a separate extension of this research.
