Project overview

A group project bringing together office interactions and gameplay systems in a top-down setting. My contributions focus on programming and level design.

Systems in the prototype

The project includes printer and email-sorting minigames, dialogue, character switching, interactions, combat and a day/night transition. These systems connect individual activities within a shared game environment.

  • Work-themed minigames provide distinct interaction tasks.
  • Dialogue and job progression connect tasks with character interactions.
  • Combat, player health and interface systems support encounter feedback.

Development status

This is an ongoing vertical slice. The systems described here are part of the developing prototype; it is not presented as a finished release.
