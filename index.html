About me

I am a Technical Game Designer and Griffith University Games Design graduate based in Queensland, Australia. My interests sit at the intersection of interactive systems, technical problem-solving and player experience. I work with Unreal Engine and Unity to develop gameplay systems, prototypes and VR experiences.

I enjoy breaking down unfamiliar technical challenges through experimentation, iteration and documentation. My approach combines practical development with a clear record of what was tested, what changed and why, helping me connect implementation decisions to the experience of the player.

My Bachelor of Cinematic Arts (Honours) research examines the design considerations involved in aligning physical objects with virtual representations for interaction in VR. This work brings together hand tracking, tangible interaction, calibration and reflective development. My research has been submitted and is awaiting assessment.

Alongside my research, I continue to develop gameplay prototypes and interactive experiences. I am interested in technical and generalist game design roles, combining gameplay systems, programming and level design. I am open to relocating internationally.

Honours research question

The work uses practice-led creative research, a Design Science Research Methodology structure and three Action Research cycles to investigate tangible interaction, physical–virtual correspondence and player experience.
