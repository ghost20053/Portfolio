About me

My interests sit at the intersection of interactive systems, technical problem-solving and player experience. I work with Unreal Engine and Unity, and I enjoy breaking down unfamiliar technical challenges through experimentation, iteration and documentation.

My Honours research examines design considerations when aligning physical objects with virtual representations in VR. Alongside my research, I develop gameplay prototypes and explore lower-level engine systems.

Honours research question

What design considerations emerge when aligning physical objects with virtual representations for interaction in VR?

The work uses practice-based creative research, a Design Science Research Methodology structure and three Action Research cycles to investigate tangible interaction, physical–virtual correspondence and player experience.
